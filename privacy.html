Gita Ghar privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

The short version

  • You can use Gita Ghar without an account. Then everything stays on your phone.
  • If you sign in, your diary, cards and progress are backed up in Amazon Web Services (AWS) in London, encrypted.
  • If you turn on smart matching, what you type is sent to AI on AWS in Europe to choose a verse. It is not saved.
  • No adverts, no tracking, no selling of data. You can delete your account at any time.

Who runs Gita Ghar

Gita Ghar is a personal, non-commercial project by Janvi Patel, based in the United Kingdom. For any privacy question or request, contact: [CONTACT EMAIL].

Information that stays on your phone

Your diary notes, cards, chapter progress, daily practice, language, Family mode and sound settings are saved in your browser's storage on your phone. They are not sent anywhere unless you sign in.

If you sign in

  • What we keep: your email address (to send you sign-in codes) and a backup of your diary notes, cards, chapter progress and practice days.
  • Where: Amazon Cognito and Amazon DynamoDB in the AWS London region (eu-west-2). Data is encrypted in storage and in transit.
  • Why: to keep your information safe and let you use it on more than one phone. The legal basis is your consent, which you give by signing in.
  • How long: until you delete your account. Deleting your account (in Account & settings) removes your backup and your sign-in details straight away. Database backups are kept for up to 35 days and then removed.

Smart matching

Smart matching is off until you agree. When it is on, the words you type into "Tell Krishna" are sent securely to Amazon Bedrock, an AI service on AWS, using data centres in the European Union. The AI only chooses which of the app's own verses fits; it does not write words for Krishna.

Gita Ghar does not save what you type, and does not write it into any log. Amazon states that Bedrock does not store these inputs or use them to train AI models. You can turn smart matching off at any time in Account & settings.

Words that suggest someone may be in danger are checked on your phone first, and helplines are shown straight away.

Other services

  • Fonts: the app loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your device's IP address when fonts are downloaded.
  • Hosting: the app is delivered by Amazon CloudFront. AWS keeps standard technical logs for security.
  • Error logs: our server logs record only the type of an error, never what you wrote. They are deleted after 14 days.

Children

Family mode is designed for children to read with an adult. Accounts are for people aged 13 and over. Children under 13 can use the app without an account, with a parent or guardian.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR), you can ask to see, correct, download or delete your information, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Contact [CONTACT EMAIL]. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Not a replacement for help

Gita Ghar shares the Gita's wisdom. It is not medical or professional advice. If things feel very heavy, please call Samaritans on 116 123 (UK) or Tele-MANAS on 14416 (India). In an emergency, call 999 (UK) or 112 (India).
